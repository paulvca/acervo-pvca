# Product purpose

Acervo PVCA is the public, navigable face of a personal film collection: a way
to open a carefully organized cinema shelf for other people to explore.
Its primary experience is discovery and consultation through a lightweight,
static publication, backed by a richer private collection.

## Visitor journey

1. Understand what the collection is on the home page.
2. Browse films visually in a grid or consult an editorial list.
3. Search by title, director or year; filter and sort the catalog.
4. Open a film page to understand both the work and the preserved copy.
5. Discover other films through personal editorial selections.
6. Follow a deliberately shared external destination to watch, download or
   consult a copy when its URL exists.

## Work and preserved copy

Film information includes title, director, year, countries, runtime, genres,
synopsis and other useful cinematic context.

Resolution, file size, audio languages, subtitles (especially Brazilian
Portuguese), and relevant edition or restoration describe the preserved copy.
These are useful public information, not administrative clutter. Select the
representative copy explicitly and keep its metadata consistent across views.
Never invent missing data. Development placeholders are not verified records.

Internet Archive and Google Drive are destinations. An intentionally shared URL
generates a link; a missing URL generates no button or availability message.
Availability is not an editorial filter, selection or central statistic.

## Public boundary

Local paths, disk structure, mounts, hashes, logs, queues, private identifiers,
credentials, QA states, upload processes, preparation states and pipeline details
remain private. Only explicitly approved public fields enter the projection.
Editorial context must help explain the films or copies rather than expose
internal operational states.

## Product decision rule

Include an interface element only when it helps a visitor discover a film,
understand the work, understand the preserved copy, or reach that copy.
Reveal the collection rather than the infrastructure maintaining it.

## Scope and identity

Keep the personal, quiet editorial identity and Flexoki Dark design. Public
navigation is Home, Films, Selections and About. Desktop and mobile are responsive
presentations of the same interface.

This is not a streaming platform, commercial service, watched-film tracker,
social network, storage dashboard, file manager or administrative interface.
Do not add accounts, login, profiles, comments, likes, ratings, stars, watchlists,
playback history, recommendation algorithms, a proprietary player, website
uploads or metadata editing. Keep Astro static and avoid unnecessary dependencies.
