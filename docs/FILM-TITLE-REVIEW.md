## Deliberate exceptions

- North of Hudson Bay (1923): retain the existing title until a Brazilian source confirms the proposed “Jornada da Morte” for this particular film. Similar titles refer to different works.
- Sicília is Michelangelo Antonioni’s 1997 short, not the Straub/Huillet feature.
- A Corte Marcial de Billy Mitchell is attested in Brazilian historical television listings; “Seu Último Comando” also appears in contemporary databases.
- Titles without a verified Brazilian release name remain as published. An untranslated title alone does not establish an error.

The source audit and complete 526-entry review are retained in the local validation directory. No credentials or private database content are included in this document.
